2. Service Description

Hebrew Time is an Android widget app that provides:

  • An analog clock with Hebrew numerals (gematria) that moves counter-clockwise
  • Integration with the Jewish calendar, including holidays and parashat ha-shavua
  • Accurate calculation of halachic times (zmanim) based on GPS location
  • Notifications for Shabbat and Jewish holidays
  • Several customizable clock themes
  • Optional premium features available via in-app purchase
