User Guide

📱 Installation & Setup

Installing the App

Download Hebrew Time from the Google Play Store and install it on your Android device (requires Android 7.0 or higher).

Adding the Widget

  • Long-press on your home screen
  • Select "Widgets" from the menu
  • Find "Hebrew Time" in the widget list
  • Drag it to your desired location on the home screen
  • The widget will automatically start displaying the current time and Hebrew date

Granting Permissions

For accurate zemanim calculation, the app needs location permission:

  • Go to Settings tab in the app
  • Tap "Enable Location" if not already granted
  • Choose "While using the app" or "Always" for best experience

⏰ Using the Zemanim Tab

The Zemanim tab displays all halachic times for the current day based on your location:

  • Alot Hashachar - Dawn (72 minutes before sunrise)
  • Misheyakir - Earliest time for tallit and tefillin
  • Sunrise (Netz) - Precise sunrise time
  • Shema (MGA/GRA) - Latest time for Shema according to different opinions
  • Tefillah (MGA/GRA) - Latest time for morning prayer
  • Chatzot - Midday (solar noon)
  • Mincha Gedolah/Ketanah - Earliest times for afternoon prayer
  • Plag Hamincha - 1.25 hours before sunset
  • Sunset (Shkiah) - Precise sunset time
  • Tzet Hakochavim - Nightfall (when stars appear)
  • Candle Lighting - 18 minutes before sunset (on Fridays/holidays)
  • Havdalah - 42 minutes after sunset (on Saturday nights/after holidays)

All times automatically update based on your GPS location and the current date.

📅 Using the Calendar Tab

Viewing the Hebrew Calendar

The Calendar tab shows a full Hebrew calendar view with:

  • Hebrew dates displayed alongside civil dates
  • Jewish holidays highlighted in color
  • Current day marked with a circle
  • Shabbat days (Saturdays) specially marked

Viewing Date Details

Tap any date to see detailed information in a bottom sheet:

  • Full Hebrew date (day, month, year)
  • Parashat Hashavua (weekly Torah portion) for Shabbat
  • Holiday names and information
  • All zemanim for that specific date
  • Candle lighting time (if applicable)
  • Havdalah time (if applicable)

Navigating the Calendar

  • Swipe left/right to change months
  • Tap the month/year header to jump to today
  • Calendar automatically adjusts for Israel vs Diaspora holiday observance

⚙️ Settings & Customization

Location Settings

  • Auto-detect Location: Uses GPS to automatically determine your location
  • Manual GPS Entry: Enter custom latitude and longitude coordinates
  • City Selection: Choose from 38 predefined cities worldwide
  • Israel/Diaspora Toggle: Manually override auto-detection (affects holiday duration and parasha schedule)

Display Settings

  • Latin Month Names: Show months in English instead of Hebrew transliteration
  • Arabic Numerals: Display day numbers using Arabic numerals (1, 2, 3...) instead of Hebrew numerals (א׳, ב׳, ג׳...)
  • Theme: Automatically follows system dark/light mode

Notification Settings

  • Enable/disable Shabbat notifications
  • Enable/disable holiday notifications
  • Notifications sent 24 hours and 30 minutes before candle lighting

❓ Troubleshooting

Widget Not Updating

  • Check if battery optimization is disabled for Hebrew Time in system settings
  • Ensure location permission is granted
  • Remove and re-add the widget to your home screen

Incorrect Zemanim

  • Verify your location is correct in Settings tab
  • Check if Israel/Diaspora setting matches your actual location
  • Ensure device time and timezone are correct

Missing Holidays or Wrong Parasha

  • Check Israel/Diaspora setting (holidays and parasha schedule differ)
  • Verify the Hebrew year in the calendar is correct

Battery Concerns

Hebrew Time is optimized for battery efficiency:

  • Updates only when screen is on
  • Uses AlarmManager for scheduled updates when screen is off
  • GPS is used only when you open the app, not continuously
  • Minimal background processing
