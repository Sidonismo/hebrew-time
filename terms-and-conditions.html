2. Description of Service

Hebrew Time is an Android widget application that provides:

  • An analog clock with Hebrew numerals (Gematria) that moves counter-clockwise
  • Jewish calendar integration with holidays and parashat hashavua
  • Precise halachic times (zemanim) calculation based on GPS location
  • Shabbat and Jewish holiday notifications
  • Multiple customizable clock skins
  • Optional premium features available via in-app purchase
